# Combat FX Free

**6 animated hit effects as PNG sprite sheets, free, for any engine.** Each comes in three sets:
256 px and 128 px HD, and pixel art drawn at 64 px, not shrunk.

### Want more?

**[Combat FX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/combat-fx)** has 60: cross cuts, thrusts, shockwaves and
blocks on top of these, and every hit in white, red, gold, blue, purple and green. Same sheets,
same player.

### What's inside

- A white **slash**, **hit** and **spark**, a red **claw** and **splat**, and a gold **crit**.
- Transparent PNG sheets, 8 frames a row, plus an `effects.json` with every frame size, frame
  count, fps and anchor, and a GIF preview of each.
- **Godot 4 player included:** `CombatFX.spawn(self, "red_claw", enemy.position)`. Import steps
  for Unity and GameMaker in the README.

### Compatibility

- **Any engine** that plays sprite sheets: Unity, GameMaker, Godot, Defold, Construct, Phaser,
  LÖVE, MonoGame.
- The Godot player: Godot **4.3 to 4.7**, tested on both ends and on every renderer.
